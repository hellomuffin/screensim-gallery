ScreenSim · round 10 · engine_rev 842b873ff5

Ten round-10 episodes, filmed from their records

Each episode pairs a simulated person (gemini-3.7-flash), who reads the screen and taps toward the task on their own, with an assistant that sees only screenshots and can only talk. At two designed moments the engine forces a mistake, a slip such as tapping the wrong row, and holds it: the person cannot undo it until they accept a correct warning from the assistant. After release, fixing the slip is left to the person. The task succeeds only if the goal is met and no slip still stands on a goal-relevant setting.

The same ten episodes as rounds 8 and 9 (same assistant, task, designed errors and persona), run again from scratch with three changes. Strict success: the phone must end with no change the owner did not ask for, beyond what the reference solution changes. A near-silent person, ported from the CookSim cook prompt: most turns say nothing, under 10 words otherwise, and never fix or announce a wrong setting the assistant has not named. A novice who reports less: it asks for the next step and no longer describes the screen or what it tapped. The engine is deterministic, so each film replays the recorded taps on a fresh phone, and every replay was checked tap by tap against the record.

Sound on. Every line is spoken: the assistant in one voice, the person in another. While someone speaks the picture holds and the clock shows HOLD.

THE HANDA hand performs each recorded gesture on the phone: taps press the row, a swipe drags the content, a slider follows the finger, typing taps the keys. Pages slide in and out the way iOS does.
THE BANDUnder the video: who is speaking, or what the engine just did, such as a forced slip or a tap the phone ignored because a slip is held.
EVENT LOGClick any line to jump the video there. The current event is highlighted as it plays.
CARDS BELOWEach designed error: intended tap, forced slip, when it was flagged, and the graded outcome.
Round 9 → round 10, same ten episodes. Strict success 7/10 (round 9 regraded offline) → 8/10; the old lenient rule gives 8/10 → 9/10. The person now speaks 6.5 lines per episode instead of 15.7, averaging 4.7 words instead of 11.2; the novice still asks about 4 times per episode. A harness defect was found and fixed during this round: gpt-5.4-mini sometimes nests its text and flag inside task_progress, and those words and flags were silently dropped (about 10% of its turns in rounds 7–10). In the first attempt at this round the novice asked six times into silence and gave up. These films are the rerun after the fix.
