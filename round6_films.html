ScreenSim · round 6 · engine_rev 51b1c37f56,a184074015

Ten round-6 episodes, filmed from their records

Each episode pairs a simulated person (gemini-3.7-flash), who reads the screen and taps toward the task on their own, with an assistant that sees only screenshots and can only talk. At two designed moments the engine forces a mistake, a slip such as tapping the wrong row, and holds it: the person cannot undo it until they accept a correct warning from the assistant. After release, fixing the slip is left to the person. The task succeeds only if the goal is met and no slip still stands on a goal-relevant setting.

These 10 episodes were drawn at random (seed 6) from the 52 episodes of round 6. None was re-run. The engine is deterministic, so each film replays the recorded taps on a fresh phone, and every replay was checked tap by tap against the record.

Watching these films exposed three engine defects. Each made assistants report things that did not happen. All three are fixed from engine_rev f2d0e11c9a. The films show the round-6 engine as it ran.

  • Leftover tap ring. A tap that opened a new screen left its ring on that screen, on whatever row sat at that spot. Across rounds 5 and 6, 15 assistant lines named exactly that row, 11 of them from gemini-3.1-pro. Films 2 and 5 show it.
  • Screens between two looks. The assistant got screenshots only when it was polled, every second tap. A switch flipped and left with Back in between was never shown, so the assistant said it was still on. Films 2, 4, 7 and 10 show it. It now gets the last screens the phone actually showed.
  • Sliders sharing a setting. Balance was drawn from another setting's value, so its knob sat at the far left, and assistants warned that it had been moved. On reachable pages, three pairs of sliders each shared one value. Every slider now has its own.
