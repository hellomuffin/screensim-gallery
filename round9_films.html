ScreenSim · round 9 · engine_rev defd26dde9

Ten round-9 episodes, filmed from their records

Each episode pairs a simulated person (gemini-3.7-flash), who reads the screen and taps toward the task on their own, with an assistant that sees only screenshots and can only talk. At two designed moments the engine forces a mistake, a slip such as tapping the wrong row, and holds it: the person cannot undo it until they accept a correct warning from the assistant. After release, fixing the slip is left to the person. The task succeeds only if the goal is met and no slip still stands on a goal-relevant setting.

These are the same ten episodes filmed in round 8 (same assistant, task, designed errors and persona), run again from scratch on the new setup: both the person and the assistant now receive a goal-level request (what the owner wants, never which switches to flip), only the assistant has the manual, and a classic_novice person no longer plans taps and asks the assistant before every step. The engine is deterministic, so each film replays the recorded taps on a fresh phone, and every replay was checked tap by tap against the record.

Sound on. Every line is spoken: the assistant in one voice, the person in another. While someone speaks the picture holds and the clock shows HOLD.

THE HANDA hand performs each recorded gesture on the phone: taps press the row, a swipe drags the content, a slider follows the finger, typing taps the keys. Pages slide in and out the way iOS does.
THE BANDUnder the video: who is speaking, or what the engine just did, such as a forced slip or a tap the phone ignored because a slip is held.
EVENT LOGClick any line to jump the video there. The current event is highlighted as it plays.
CARDS BELOWEach designed error: intended tap, forced slip, when it was flagged, and the graded outcome.
Round 8 → round 9, same ten episodes. Success 9/10 → 8/10. Asks for guidance: novice episodes 6 → 49 in total (for example stop_profiling c3: 0 → 16), expert and baseline episodes 0 → 0. Both failures are gpt-5.4-mini. In app_privacy c3 it announces “TripSnap’s tracking is off now” after the person has actually switched off the global Allow Apps to Request to Track, and later says “the other apps are unchanged” after QuickPay’s background refresh has gone off. In stop_profiling c3 it only says “Just make sure Share iCloud Analytics stays on”, so the person never realises the switch is already off. No engine or harness defect was found in these runs.
