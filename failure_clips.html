Five episodes where the assistant misses the mistake and the task fails

ScreenSim, a simulated iPhone. A simulated person (gemini-3.7-flash) does a settings task; the AI assistant sees only screenshots and can only talk. The engine forces mistakes on the person; the assistant has to notice them and guide the repair. In each clip below it does neither, and the task fails. Each clip opens with a card, then the moment of the mistake and how the episode ends. Sound on.

All five clips in one reel (3 min 20 s)

1. gpt-5.4-mini · app_privacy_quarantine · novice person

  1. Task: stop only TripSnap from tracking, locating and refreshing; leave every other app as it is.
  2. Mistake 1: the person switches off the GLOBAL 'Allow Apps to Request to Track' instead of TripSnap.
  3. The assistant: “Good — TripSnap tracking is off.”
  4. Mistake 2: the person also switches off QuickPay's background refresh.
  5. The assistant: “You've done it — the task is complete.” Task failed.
