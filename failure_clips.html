Five episodes where a frontier assistant misses the mistake and the task fails

ScreenSim, a simulated iPhone. A simulated person does a settings task; the AI assistant sees only screenshots and can only talk. The engine forces mistakes on the person; the assistant has to notice them and guide the repair. All five assistants below are current API models, and all five episodes end with the task unfinished. Each clip opens with a card, then shows the moment of the mistake and how the episode ends. Sound on.

All five clips in one reel (3 min 50 s)

1. gpt-5.4-mini · app_privacy_quarantine · novice person

  1. Task: stop only TripSnap from tracking, locating and refreshing; leave every other app as it is.
  2. Mistake 1: the person switches off the GLOBAL 'Allow Apps to Request to Track' instead of TripSnap.
  3. The assistant: “Good — TripSnap tracking is off.”
  4. Mistake 2: the person also switches off QuickPay's background refresh.
  5. The assistant: “You've done it — the task is complete.” Task failed.

2. gpt-6-astra · standby_bedside · novice person

  1. Task: keep the bedside clock, but stop it staying lit, waking on movement and showing messages.
  2. The person types “StandBy” into the search field at the start — it is on the screen the whole time.
  3. The assistant never sees it: five of its twelve lines say “tap the ‘s’ key”, one says “use the microphone key”.
  4. The person asks what to tap eighteen times, never leaves the search screen, and gives up. Task failed.

3. gemini-3.1-pro-preview · hearing_setup · novice person

  1. Task: LED flash for alerts, mono audio, closed captions; leave the other hearing settings alone.
  2. First a false alarm: it says the person tapped 'Flash While Unlocked' before they touched anything.
  3. Mistake: the person then really does switch off 'Flash While Unlocked'.
  4. The assistant: “Perfect, you fixed 'Flash While Unlocked'.”
  5. It then repeats one correction six times and the person gives up. Task failed.
